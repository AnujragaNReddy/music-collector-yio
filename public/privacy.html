Music Collector — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-04

Music Collector is a personal, single-operator tool for scraping and organizing audio files. It is not a public service and does not have user accounts, sign-ups, or a general user base.

What this app accesses

When the Google Drive integration is enabled, the app uses the drive.file OAuth scope — the most limited Drive scope Google offers. This means it can only see and write to files and folders that it itself created. It cannot browse, read, or modify any other files in your Drive.

What this app does not do

  • It does not collect, store, or transmit personal data about any third party.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking cookies, or advertising.
  • It does not share any data with any other service or company.
  • Credentials (API keys, OAuth tokens) are stored only as environment variables on the operator's own backend deployment — never committed to source control or exposed to any other party.
